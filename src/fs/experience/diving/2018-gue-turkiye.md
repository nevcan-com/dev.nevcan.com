# Founder & regional director · GUE Türkiye

**Since 2018** · Istanbul · [gue.com.tr](https://gue.com.tr)

Global Underwater Explorers' official representative in Türkiye: a volunteer community of divers
running education, conservation and exploration projects.

- Started the [Campus Program](https://gue.com.tr/campus-program/en.html) in 2026: student
  ambassadors at universities including Middle East Technical University, Işık, Süleyman Demirel
  and Sabancı connect their communities with GUE Türkiye for exploration, conservation and
  awareness projects
- Started a small student scholarship of our own, independent of GUE's: one student a year gets
  free GUE training
- Built its websites and tools: [gue.com.tr](https://gue.com.tr), earlier gueturkey.com, and
  [GUE TR Hub](../../projects/gue-tr-hub.md)

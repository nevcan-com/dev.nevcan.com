# Volunteer, software and marketing · Global Underwater Explorers

**Since 2022** · remote · [gue.com](https://www.gue.com)

Global Underwater Explorers (GUE) is a nonprofit diving organization for education,
conservation and exploration. I've taught for GUE since 2022, as an instructor trainer since
2026, and I volunteer for its headquarters: I take problems the organization has and build what
solves them, from software to new programs. Since 2026 I'm on the marketing team.

- Built software for GUE's own problems: [Peyk Signal](../../projects/peyk-signal.md) for the
  marketing team, [GUE TR Hub](../../projects/gue-tr-hub.md) for instructors,
  [Caveline](../../projects/caveline.md) for cave surveys, and a landing page for the GUE
  community.
- Since 2025, run Fitness Month, GUE's yearly training month: its management and
  communication, and its move online, with a platform that tracks participation.
- Review GUE's material before release: on the pre-review team for GUE Standards v9 and v10,
  finding and reporting contradictions; an early adopter of GUE Mastery Learning, its adaptive
  learning platform, reporting errors in its courses; and a contributor to the Gas Blender
  course documentation.
- For GUE TV, standardized the authors section and produced a Turkish video on setting up
  doubles.
- The [Campus Program](2018-gue-tr.md) I started at GUE Türkiye is now spreading to
  other countries.
- Also: a model on GUE's 2022 creativity trip in Egypt, a volunteer adviser for GUE
  scholarships since 2022, and smaller parts in the 2022 Project Diver conference, the
  Performance Diver and Project Diver courses, and the last months of GUE's old Academy
  platform.

Most of the software lives under [GUE.one](https://gue.one), my own playground, not an
official GUE site.

# Education

## MA, Mediterranean Underwater Cultural Heritage Research

**Akdeniz University** · 2025–2027

Thesis, in progress: _Typological Analysis of Mediterranean Amphorae Using Artificial
Intelligence_. Its prototype, Kaide, uses computer vision to suggest an amphora's type from a
photo taken in the field or underwater, reading the rim, neck, handles, body and base. Instead
of matching a find by eye against pages of catalogues, researchers get a fast first answer with
less room for human error.

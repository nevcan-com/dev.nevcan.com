# Cookies

_Last updated 20 November 2026_

This site stores very little in your browser. Analytics cookies are set only after you choose
"Accept"; nothing else needs your consent.

| Name               | Type          | What for                                    | How long  | Consent    |
| ------------------ | ------------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------- | ---------- |
| dev-consent        | local storage | Remembers your cookie choice                | 12 months | Not needed |
| dev-theme          | local storage | Remembers the color theme you picked        | 12 months | Not needed |
| dev-user           | local storage | Remembers the name you logged in with       | 12 months | Not needed |
| dev_ga             | cookie        | Google Analytics: tells visitors apart      | 2 years   | Yes        |
| dev_ga\_&lt;id&gt; | cookie        | Google Analytics: keeps the session's state | 2 years   | Yes        |

All of them belong to dev.nevcan.com only; they are not shared with the other nevcan.com sites.

**Not used here:** advertising cookies, social media buttons or embeds, third-party fonts.

## Changing your mind

Open [Cookie settings](#cookie-settings) (also "Cookies" in the status bar, or type `cookies` in the
terminal). Withdrawing consent stops Google Analytics and removes the dev_ga cookies. You can also
delete cookies and site data in your browser at any time.

How the data is processed, and your rights: [privacy.md](privacy.md).

# Privacy

_Last updated 20 November 2026_

## In short

- **Cookies only with your consent.** Analytics cookies are set only if you accept them.
- **Analytics, not advertising.** Google Analytics shows which pages are visited. No advertising
  pixels or advertising features, and Analytics data isn't shared with Google for its own
  purposes.
- **What you type stays in your browser.** The terminal runs entirely in your browser. Commands
  you type are not sent or stored anywhere, and the history is gone when you close the page.
- **Your choices are remembered.** Your cookie choice, and a colour theme or a username if you
  choose one, are stored in your browser.
- **Served from this site.** Fonts and images are served by this site's host, GitHub Pages (no
  third-party fonts or embeds); Google Analytics loads from Google only after you accept.

You are asked before any analytics cookie is set, and you can change your choice at any time under
[Cookie settings](#cookie-settings), "Cookies" in the status bar, or by typing `cookies` in the
terminal. Every cookie is listed in [cookies.md](cookies.md).

## Analytics

With your consent (KVKK Art. 5(1); GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), this site uses Google Analytics 4 to see
which pages are visited and how. The data is collected automatically by a script and cookies, only
after you accept. Google processes the pages viewed, device and browser type and an approximate
location derived from your IP address. For visitors in the EU, Google says it does not log or store
IP addresses. Its cookies (dev_ga, dev_ga_…) last up to two years; detailed data linked to the
cookie is deleted after 14 months; aggregated page-view totals are kept. Commands typed in the
terminal are not sent to Google.

Recipient: Google (Google Ireland Ltd and Google LLC), as the analytics provider. This is a
transfer abroad, including to the US, on the basis of your explicit consent to this transfer (KVKK
Art. 9(6)(a)), which you give by choosing "Accept" after being told the risks: the Personal Data
Protection Board has not decided that the US provides adequate protection, no standard contract
approved by the Board is in place with Google, and US authorities may be able to access the data
under US law. For visitors in the EU: the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in Google's data
processing terms (GDPR Art. 46(2)(c)).

You can withdraw consent at any time under [Cookie settings](#cookie-settings). Withdrawing stops
further collection and removes the dev_ga cookies; it doesn't affect processing before you
withdrew.

Your choice itself is stored in your browser (local storage entry "dev-consent", kept for 12
months, after which you are asked again). This is needed to respect your choice and needs no
consent. A colour theme you pick is stored the same way ("dev-theme", 12 months); it never leaves
your browser and needs no consent. A username you log in with is kept the same way ("dev-user",
12 months): it is never sent anywhere, analytics included, and `logout` deletes it.

## Your rights

Under KVKK Article 11 you can apply to the data controller to: (a) learn whether your personal data
is processed; (b) if so, request information about it; (c) learn the purpose of the processing and
whether the data is used for that purpose; (ç) know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom
it is transferred; (d) have incomplete or inaccurate data corrected; (e) have it deleted or
destroyed under the conditions of KVKK Article 7; (f) have the third parties it was transferred to
told of a correction or deletion under (d) or (e); (g) object to a result against you that arises
solely from automated analysis of your data; (ğ) claim compensation if you suffer damage from
unlawful processing. Where processing is based on your consent (analytics), you can withdraw it at
any time under Cookie settings. If the GDPR applies to you, you also have the rights in GDPR
Articles 15–22 (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection) and may
withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)).

If your request is refused, the answer is inadequate or there is no answer in time, you can
complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (KVK Kurulu) within 30 days of learning the answer,
and in any case within 60 days of your application. You must apply to the controller first. If you
are in the EU, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

# Caveline

**Since 2023** · prototype

A video player for cave surveys, where the survey line is the timeline. Cave-dive footage runs
long, and finding one part of the cave means scrubbing through it blind; with Caveline you click
a point on the survey line and the player jumps there.

- A cave modeled as a graph of stations and branches, not a straight line
- One or several videos bound to it

Status: prototype. It draws the cave line from survey data (line length, depth and compass
bearing at each station); binding the footage to it is next.

# Caveline

**Since 2023** · prototype

A video player for cave surveys, where the survey line is the timeline. Instead of a straight
scrubber, you click a point on the line and the player jumps to the footage of that part of the
cave. A cave is a graph of stations and branches, and one or several videos can be bound to it.

Status: prototype. It draws the cave line from survey data (line length, depth and compass
bearing at each station); binding the footage to it is next.

Built for GUE: [experience/diving/2022-gue](../experience/diving/2022-gue.md).

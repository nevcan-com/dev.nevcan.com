# Dipnot

**Since 2025** · technical co-founder · [dipnot.app](https://dipnot.app)

A Turkish publication on diving, underwater photography and marine life. Good material for
divers in Türkiye is mostly in other languages, and what exists in Turkish is scarce and
scattered; Dipnot gathers it in one place, in Turkish. The name means "footnote", with a pun on
_dip_, the seabed.

- Articles and news for readers
- Members save articles, follow the categories they care about and get notifications

More than 4,500 members; the app is on the App Store and Google Play.

# WetNote

**Since 2026** · in development

My underwater software under one name: one brand, many modules, each a separate app with its
own stack. New tools join as modules over time.

## Kaide

The first module, and the subject of my master's thesis. Upload a photo of an amphora, taken in
the field or underwater, and Kaide uses computer vision to suggest its type from the rim, neck,
handles, body and base. Instead of matching a find by eye against pages of catalogs,
researchers get a fast first answer with less room for human error. Status: prototype.

More on the thesis: [education](../education.md).

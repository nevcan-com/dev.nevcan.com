# GUE.ONE

**Since 2026** · [gue.one](https://gue.one) · every GUE project, one global community

A home for the projects I build for the Global Underwater Explorers community:

- **Fitness Month**: every August, GUE divers around the world train together. Not a
  competition: a shared month of training and accountability that keeps the community ready
  for the water. [fitness.gue.one](https://fitness.gue.one)
- **Signal**: surfaces the social-media conversations that matter, so GUE's marketing team can
  amplify what the community is already saying. Built on [Peyk Signal](peyk-signal.md).
- **Cave Sync**, coming soon: an interactive cave map paired with dive footage. Click a passage
  on the survey line and the video jumps to that part of the dive.

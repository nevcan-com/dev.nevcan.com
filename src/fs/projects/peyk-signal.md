# Peyk Signal

**Since 2026** · a VAC Studio product · [signal.getpeyk.com](https://signal.getpeyk.com)

An attention filter for social media. Signal watches connected accounts, classifies every
incoming message with Claude, scores it from 0 to 10, and sends a Telegram alert only when
something needs a person; the rest waits in a review queue or is archived. Multi-tenant: every
workspace sees only its own data.

Live for Global Underwater Explorers, its first user; a SaaS version for other teams is
planned.

Built with Python, FastAPI and the Claude API, on Firestore, with encryption keys in Google
Cloud KMS.

Signal is one of two products under the Peyk name; the other, Inbox, is a partner's.

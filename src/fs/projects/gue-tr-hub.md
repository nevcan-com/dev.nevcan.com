# GUE TR Hub

**Since 2026** · [hub.gue.com.tr](https://hub.gue.com.tr)

A reference hub for GUE instructors and instructor candidates. Teaching a GUE course means
working from standards, SOPs, course plans and drill descriptions spread across many PDFs; the
hub brings them together, organized by course, so everything a course needs is in one place.

- A page for each course, from Fundamentals to Cave 3 and CCR: schedules, drills and the
  standards it cites
- Drill pages with the steps, the courses that teach them and the SOP section they follow
- A library of every document, filterable and linkable, with search across the whole hub
- E-mail templates, and personal notes on each course and drill that only their author sees

Every page sits behind Google sign-in: GUE addresses register themselves, candidates are added
by invitation.

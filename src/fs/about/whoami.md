# whoami

**Nevcan Uludaş** · Software architect · Technical co-founder of [Dipnot](../projects/dipnot.md)

I've been building for the web since 2006, across startups, large e-commerce platforms and
enterprise products: D&R's online stores, Doğuş Group's products at Doğuş Teknoloji, and Perfx,
a performance management platform, at SabancıDx. Most of that work was in Scrum teams, side by
side with managers, designers and developers, and along the way front-end architecture became
my speciality.

Today I'm the technical co-founder of Dipnot, a diving and marine-life publication with a mobile
app, an admin platform and a website. I own it end to end, from architecture and security to
delivery, as a one-person engineering team working with AI coding agents.

Diving runs alongside all of it. I've been diving since 2008 and teaching since 2011, full time
since 2022, when I joined Deep Dive Dubai, the world's deepest pool; there I also built the
small tools the operation needed. I was Türkiye's first GUE instructor and am now the only GUE
instructor trainer in Türkiye.

My master's thesis at Akdeniz University brings both sides together: classifying Mediterranean
amphorae with artificial intelligence.

More at [dive.nevcan.com](https://dive.nevcan.com).
